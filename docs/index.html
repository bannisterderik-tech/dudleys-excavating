We know how the ground moves here

Dudley’s Excavating has been serving the Gerber area for generations. We understand the way the ground moves around here, the soil types that are common, and the hazards a project can run into — because we've been digging in it our whole lives.

  • Three generations of Dudleys running the work — Scott Dudley, Michael Dudley, Kyle Dudley.
