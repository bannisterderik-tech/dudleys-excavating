Northern California doesn't give you clean digging. Mountain grades give you granite; the valley gives you hardpan and cobble. We cut it, plate it overnight, and keep the road open while we work the trench.

  • Trenching in soil, cobble and rock — shored and plated to spec.
  • Roadside excavation with our own certified traffic control (it's on our license).
  • Steep-ground work — hillside cuts with full rigging and spotters.
  • Radio-dispatched hauling — spoils out and base rock in without waiting on a sub.
